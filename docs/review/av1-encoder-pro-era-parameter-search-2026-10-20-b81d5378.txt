AV1 ENCODER PRO - bounded parameter search, an idea taken from ERA
2026-10-20

Sources:
- https://github.com/google-research/era @ 012c2d3
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06503
Source-only review: nothing installed or run. Proposed use is conceptual,
not checked against the AV1_Encoder_Pro repo.

TAKE:
- ERA's loop - generate a candidate, execute it, score it, let tree search
  pick what to try next - fits encoder tuning well because encoding is a
  scorable task: quality metric, encode time and file size are all
  measurable.
- Proposed use: search SVT-AV1 parameter sets (preset / CRF / tune
  combinations, or candidate flag sets) against measured quality (e.g.
  VMAF/SSIM), runtime and output size.
- The reusable core is implementation/futs.py (~100 lines, Apache-2.0): flat
  tree search, PUCT selection, visit-count backprop, answer = best raw
  score.
- A portable detail: converting raw scores to ranks (rank/(n-1)) before
  combining with the exploration bonus reduces dependence on a metric's
  numerical score range. It does not settle how quality, time and size
  should be weighted against each other - that choice is yours.
- No LLM required for this use: the search works with any candidate
  generator. A plain parameter sampler over the flag space is a fine
  generate step.

DISCIPLINE REQUIRED:
- The search optimises exactly what you score. Score VMAF alone and it will
  buy VMAF with bitrate; put size and time in the objective if they matter.
- Keep a held-out set of clips for final evaluation that the search never
  sees, so reported gains aren't overfit to the tuning corpus.

COST REALITY:
- Each candidate costs one full encode plus one metric pass, and what that
  costs depends on clip duration, encoder settings and your hardware. Time a
  small sample of encodes first and set the search budget from that
  measurement - no runtime promise is made here.

DO NOT TAKE:
- The ERA repo as-is: its sandbox is a deliberate stub and the shipped
  example is broken (confirmed TypeErrors, README/code mismatches). Reuse
  futs.py with your own generate/execute/score functions.
